Client Declined is the one worth acting on. These are real families who engaged with a paid ad, entered the funnel, and then said no — not spam, not a service mismatch, not an insurance issue. That's the definition of a regrettable loss: demand that existed and was lost somewhere in the follow-up, pricing conversation, or timing, rather than lost to fit.

At 31 leads (~5% of all 636 paid leads this quarter), even a modest win-back rate matters: recovering just 20% of Client Declined leads would mean ~6 additional clients and roughly $335,000 in additional annual revenue, against ad spend that's already been paid for. Recommend a re-engagement touchpoint (call/email cadence) specifically for this decline reason before writing these leads off.
